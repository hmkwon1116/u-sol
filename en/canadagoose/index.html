Canada Goose picks.

A guide to picking the one jacket you actually need for a Korean winter. Compare every model on TEI warmth rating, down fill power, price and sizing in one place.

📅— 🧊8 models 🌡️TEI 2 – 5

🌡️ TEI (warmth rating) — which level should you buy?

TEI 20 to -5°C · early winter and shoulder-season daily wear, for city days in and out of buildings
TEI 3-5 to -15°C · the best-balanced band for an average Seoul winter
TEI 4-15 to -25°C · cold-wave warnings, the Gangwon region, long stretches outdoors
TEI 5Below -25°C · polar spec. Overkill for most Korean cities
How to choose
🏙️ One jacket for a Seoul commute

If you are moving between the subway and heated buildings, TEI 4 and up is actually too warm. A length that covers the hips is the most versatile.

  • First choice — Chilliwack Bomber (short cut, easy in a car)
  • Second — Langford Parka (thigh length, fine over a suit)
  • Layering — one knit underneath is enough; a thick hoodie collapses the silhouette.
🏔️ One jacket for cold snaps and the outdoors

If you have to stand around below -15°C, length and a hood ruff (fur trim) change how cold it feels more than anything else.

  • First choice — Expedition Parka (TEI 5, the original, built for Antarctic station crew)
  • Second — Kensington Parka (women's long cut, covers to above the knee)
  • Careful — if you are mostly indoors it makes you sweat, which ends up feeling colder.
📏 Sizing and care
  • Sizing — true to size on the whole. It is not an Asian fit, though, so the shoulders run generous — slimmer builds look cleaner one size down.
  • Washing — no dry cleaning. Hand-wash alone in lukewarm water or use a down cycle, then dry it completely.
  • Storage — never store it compressed. Left crushed, the down dies and the warmth does not come back.
  • Authenticity — the hologram label inside the left-arm badge carries a unique serial you can look up on the official site.
Where to buy
🛒 Official, department stores, parallel imports

Full price sits with the official store and the department stores; last season stock is cheaper at outlets and through parallel importers. With parallel imports, always check the serial can be looked up.

  • Official — canadagoose.com (pick country and language, then the Korean store)
  • Authorised in Korea — counters in the main Shinsegae, Lotte and Hyundai department stores, plus their official online channels
  • Price comparison — Naver Shopping · Google Shopping
  • When to buy — the end-of-season sales in late January and February cut last season models the hardest.

Prices are rough reference figures that move with the season, the colour and the exchange rate. Check the actual price with the seller.
